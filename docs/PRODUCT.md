# Politidle

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A satirical alternate-history game: appoint randomly offered figures to cabinet roles, then see how the cabinet handles a historical crisis.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed by the user: six fresh candidates every round; appoint one to any vacant role.
- The existing engine has five roles, one reshuffle, one individual replacement, daily and free-play seeds, a narrated resolution, and a score verdict.
- Preserve the existing roster, stats, satire, scoring and special run-ending character behavior.
- The audience and publication plans remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

The user’s sketch sets the composition: a board on the left, names and portraits pinned to it, draggable role placement, and a world map on the right. The user replaced their initial coffee-cup idea with a wall-map direction, then requested a red circle showing where each event happens. Product name: Politidle.

## Evidence on Hand

`src/engine/` implements gameplay; `content/` contains 122 reviewed figures and historical events. The user supplied a desk-and-board drawing. No existing portrait assets were present at the start of the redesign.
